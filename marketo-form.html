Marketo form

Runs the integration script from the How to identify Marketo form submitters guide against a stand-in for the liferay.com demo form — same Marketo form id and field names, hidden campaign fields included. The script is loaded as a plain <script> and left entirely alone: it wires itself to MktoForms2 and navigates with location.href, exactly as it will on a customer page. Submitting therefore performs a real navigation; the harness comes back and reports what happened on the way out.
