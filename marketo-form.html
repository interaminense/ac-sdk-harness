Marketo form

Runs the integration script from the How to identify Marketo form submitters guide against a stand-in for the liferay.com demo form — same Marketo form id and field names, hidden campaign fields included. The script is loaded as a plain <script> and left entirely alone: it wires itself to MktoForms2 and navigates with location.href — calling Analytics.setIdentity({fields}) and nothing else — exactly as it will on a customer page. Submitting therefore performs a real navigation; the harness comes back and reports what happened on the way out.

Scenarios

The first two differ in where the submit sends the visitor; the last two differ in who the visitor already is. Each performs a real submission through the script.

Stays on the site lands on a page of this harness, so anything still queued would have a later chance to go out. Redirects off the site leaves for liferay.com — a different origin, where the queue can never be drained, so the request has to have left already. It runs in an embedded copy of this page, so the departure is real but you stay here and see the result straight away. Anonymous starts with no identity at all; Known signs a visitor in first, then submits the form under a different address.
